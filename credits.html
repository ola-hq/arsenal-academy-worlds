Classroom privacy

The draft uses numbers 1–21. Names, student images and student records are never requested. Draft order, player exploration, teacher screening marks and vault results exist only in page memory; reloading or closing the experience clears them. There is no analytics, account, form submission or browser storage. Hosting providers receive normal page requests.

All journal images load locally. Cinema opens official videos externally because the video owner disables embedded playback. No third-party video player or thumbnail loads inside this site. Official-source links and player-highlight links open external services. Teacher screening is required for the exact material, captions, ads and autoplay before projection.

Player profiles reflect the supplied physical card set and are not a live squad list. Digital exploration does not reserve physical cards. Manage student names and physical stock offline.
