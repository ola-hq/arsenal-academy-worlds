# Arsenal Academy Worlds 🔴⚪

A standalone, static, classroom-friendly Arsenal fan experience.

## Included
- Interactive home / world map
- Nine classroom story slides with speaking notes
- Scouting profiles of 20 Arsenal footballers
- England flag rendered with SVG rather than an unreliable emoji
- Teacher-preview-only official video links
- Sequential six-badge captain ceremony: two Tony Adams, two Sol Campbell, two Arsène Wenger, each with a distinct story

## Classroom parameters
42 physical cards, 21 students, six captain badge cards. Physical stock and student selections are managed offline. The site does not store student names, images, or classroom records.

## Local preview
Open `index.html` in a browser; there is no build step or package installation.

## GitHub Pages
In repository Settings → Pages, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/(root)`, then Save. The expected URL is:

https://ola-hq.github.io/arsenal-academy-worlds/

Publishing is intentionally left to the repository owner. Do not mark videos classroom-cleared until the teacher has watched the exact footage, audio, captions and ads.

## Notes
Independent educational fan project. Not affiliated with Arsenal FC. External video and club materials remain the property of their respective owners. The site does not embed, download or redistribute third-party footage.
